# ERP Modules

## Sales and customer orders

Customer masters, salespeople, multi-product Customer Orders (C/O), order value calculation, dispatch position, protected edits, deletion requests, and PO/stock allocation.

## Purchase and vendors

Vendor onboarding, supporting documents, purchase-order preparation, workflow status, printable output, and approval controls.

## Production and inventory

Production orders, daily updates, serial imports, packing, pallet construction, warehouse stock, reservations, and traceability.

## Accounts and logistics

Customer invoices, receipts, outstanding balances, transporters, vehicles, freight settlement, dispatch records, and challan history.

## Administration

Users, departments, designations, granular rights, request routes, notifications, system logs, audit history, and operational health monitoring.

## Employee operations

Attendance integration, employee tours, expense claims, recharge reimbursements, receipts, and department-based review.
